About me

A lifelong gamer from sunny Malta, I have a variety of gaming interests, which are mainly focused on PC, Mobile and a handful console games. Being brought up with a trusty Gameboy, I also enjoy a fair few handheld games on platforms such as; the Nintendo 3DS series and the Switch.

In terms of games, my taste varies and is mostly dependant on the game; the XCOM series, the Warcraft series and League of Legends are amongst my favorites. In addition to this, I enjoy playing a mixture of Jam games with a particular interest in innovative games or games with fresh ideas.
